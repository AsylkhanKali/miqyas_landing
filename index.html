Why Miqyas

Not a black box.
A measurement.

Most tools give you photos and a score. Miqyas gives you a number tied to your model, reproducible on demand, written in terms your PM and your lender already understand.

Auditable & deterministic

Re-run it. Get the same answer.

The same site image always produces byte-identical output — guaranteed by a content-hash cache over a pinned local model with fixed-seed, greedy decoding. When a number drives a payment claim or a dispute, you can reproduce it on demand.

Same photo → same answer, always
BIM-grounded

Every % maps to an element.

Not dated photos. Each progress figure traces to a specific model element, a specific schedule activity, and the exact frame it was observed in — so you can always see where a number came from.

Standard language

Reads like a normal report.

Outputs speak in Earned Value and progress terms your PMs and owners already use. No new vocabulary, no black-box score to explain in the owner meeting.

Features

Under
the hood.

01

AI Deviation Detection

Computer vision identifies elements across 13 construction categories. IoU scoring flags behind, ahead, and not-started elements automatically — no manual annotations needed.

02

Critical Path Analysis

Deviations cross-referenced with your P6 schedule. A slip on a float-rich element is noise. The same slip on the core wall is a crisis. We tell you which is which.

03

Interactive 3D BIM Viewer

Real IFC geometry rendered in the browser — not bounding boxes. Click any element to see its IoU score, deviation status, and linked schedule activity.

04

Procore Integration

Connect via OAuth2 in under 2 minutes. Configure field mapping once, then bulk-push deviations as RFIs or Issues in one click. Full audit trail on both sides.
